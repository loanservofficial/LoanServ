## Why Businesses Borrow

Every growing business eventually needs capital beyond what it generates internally, whether to buy inventory, upgrade machinery, hire staff, expand to a new location or simply smooth out cash-flow gaps between paying suppliers and collecting from customers. A business loan provides that capital in a structured way, letting you seize opportunities without draining your reserves or diluting ownership.

In India, business loans are offered by banks, NBFCs and fintech lenders to proprietorships, partnerships, LLPs, private limited companies and even self-employed professionals. Amounts range from a few lakhs to several crores, and both secured and unsecured options exist. Compare tailored offers on our [Business Loan](/loans/business-loan) page.

## Types of Business Loans

Choosing the right product matters as much as getting approved. Common types include:

- **Term loan:** A lump sum repaid over a fixed tenure with EMIs, ideal for expansion, equipment or one-time investments.
- **Working capital loan:** Short-term funding to manage day-to-day operations and cash-flow cycles.
- **Overdraft / cash credit:** A revolving limit against which you draw as needed and pay interest only on the amount used.
- **Equipment or machinery finance:** Secured against the asset being purchased.
- **Loan against property:** A secured loan using commercial or residential property as collateral, offering large amounts at lower rates. See our [Loan Against Property](/loans/loan-against-property) page.
- **Invoice or bill discounting:** Advances against unpaid customer invoices to unlock tied-up cash.

Matching the product to the purpose keeps your cost down and your repayment aligned with how the money generates returns.

## Eligibility Criteria

Business loan eligibility looks at both you and your business.

- **Business vintage:** Most lenders want 2 to 3 years of operational history, though some fintechs fund newer businesses.
- **Turnover:** A minimum annual turnover, often Rs. 10 lakh to Rs. 40 lakh, backed by bank statements and GST filings.
- **Profitability:** Consistent profits over recent years strengthen your case.
- **Credit score:** Both the promoter's personal [CIBIL score](/blog/what-is-a-good-credit-score-in-india) and the business credit report (CIBIL Rank / CMR) are checked. A score above 750 and a healthy CMR help.
- **Age:** Promoters typically need to be between 21 and 65 years.

## Interest Rates and Charges

Unsecured business loan rates in India generally range from about 14 percent to 26 percent per annum, while secured loans like loan against property can be significantly lower, often 9-14 percent. Your rate depends on business vintage, turnover, profitability, credit profile and whether collateral is offered.

Watch for these charges:

| Charge | Typical Range |
| --- | --- |
| Processing fee | 1% to 3% of loan amount |
| Prepayment / foreclosure | 0% to 5% of outstanding |
| Penal interest on late EMI | 2% to 3% per month |
| Documentation / legal charges | Varies |
| GST | 18% on fees |

Always compare the effective annual cost, not just the headline rate, and model repayment on our [EMI calculator](/calculators/emi-calculator).

## Documents Required

Business loan documentation is heavier than for personal loans because lenders assess the enterprise as well as the individual.

- KYC of promoters: PAN, Aadhaar, address proof
- Business registration proof: GST certificate, shop licence, partnership deed or certificate of incorporation
- Last 2-3 years ITRs with computation and audited financials
- Last 6-12 months business bank statements
- GST returns for the recent period
- Ownership proof of business premises or collateral, if secured

## Managing Working Capital

Many businesses fail not from lack of profit but from lack of cash at the wrong moment. Working capital finance bridges the gap between outgoing payments and incoming receipts. A cash-credit or overdraft facility is often better than a term loan for this purpose because you pay interest only on what you actually use, and the limit revolves as you repay. Discipline is key: use the facility for genuine operational needs, not to mask structural losses, and clear it periodically to keep your cost low and your limit healthy.

## Government Schemes Worth Knowing

The Indian government backs several schemes to make credit accessible to small businesses:

- **MUDRA loans:** Collateral-free loans up to Rs. 10 lakh under Shishu, Kishor and Tarun categories for micro and small enterprises.
- **CGTMSE:** Credit guarantee cover that lets banks lend to micro and small enterprises without collateral.
- **Stand-Up India:** Loans for SC/ST and women entrepreneurs to set up greenfield enterprises.
- **PMEGP:** Subsidy-linked funding for new micro-enterprises.

If your business qualifies, these schemes can lower your cost and reduce collateral demands substantially. Ask lenders whether your application can be routed through an applicable scheme.

## Secured vs Unsecured: Which to Choose

An unsecured business loan is faster and needs no collateral, but carries a higher rate and smaller ticket size. A secured loan, such as loan against property, offers larger amounts, longer tenures and lower rates, at the cost of pledging an asset and a slower approval. As a rule, use unsecured loans for short-term, smaller needs where speed matters, and secured loans for large, long-term investments where the lower rate justifies the collateral.

## Borrowing Responsibly

- **Borrow against a plan, not a hope:** Every rupee borrowed should have a clear path to generating a return greater than its interest cost.
- **Match tenure to purpose:** Fund short-term needs with short-term credit and long-term assets with long-term loans.
- **Protect your credit:** Timely repayment builds your business credit profile, unlocking cheaper and larger loans later. Late payments do lasting damage; learn to monitor them via our [how to read your credit report](/knowledge-center/tutorials/how-to-read-your-credit-report) guide.
- **Keep documentation clean:** Well-maintained GST filings, ITRs and bank statements dramatically speed up approvals.
