## Who Can Get a Business Loan in India?

A business loan can be the difference between letting an opportunity pass and scaling up. Yet many small business owners in Hyderabad, Vijayawada and Bengaluru get rejected simply because they applied to the wrong lender or misunderstood the eligibility rules. This guide breaks down exactly what Indian banks and NBFCs look for so you can apply with confidence.

Whether you run a kirana store, a manufacturing unit, a services firm or a trading business, most lenders assess the same core parameters. Understanding them upfront saves you a hard enquiry on your credit report and weeks of back-and-forth.

## Core Eligibility Parameters

Lenders weigh a mix of business and personal factors. Here are the ones that matter most.

**Business vintage.** Most banks want to see at least 2-3 years of continuous operations. NBFCs and fintech lenders are more flexible and may fund businesses with just 12 months of vintage, though usually at a higher interest rate.

**Annual turnover.** A minimum turnover of ₹10 lakh to ₹40 lakh per year is common for unsecured loans. Higher turnover unlocks larger sanctions and better rates.

**Profitability.** Your business should typically be profitable for at least the last one or two financial years, as shown in your Income Tax Returns and audited financials.

**Age of the applicant.** The proprietor or key promoter should usually be between 21 and 65 years at loan maturity.

**Credit score.** A CIBIL score of 700 or above significantly improves approval odds and pricing. Both your personal and, where applicable, your business (CIBIL Rank / CMR) scores are checked.

### Typical Eligibility Snapshot

| Parameter | Banks | NBFCs / Fintech |
|---|---|---|
| Business vintage | 3+ years | 1+ year |
| Minimum turnover | ₹40 lakh | ₹10 lakh |
| CIBIL score | 700+ | 650+ |
| Loan amount | ₹5 lakh - ₹5 crore | ₹50,000 - ₹75 lakh |
| Collateral | Often required above ₹25-50 lakh | Usually unsecured |

Figures are indicative and vary by lender and profile.

## Documents You Will Need

Keeping your paperwork ready speeds up sanction. A standard checklist includes:

- **KYC documents** — PAN and Aadhaar of proprietor/partners/directors
- **Business proof** — GST registration, Udyam (MSME) certificate, Shop & Establishment licence or trade licence
- **Financials** — Last 2 years ITR with computation, audited balance sheet and profit & loss statement
- **Bank statements** — Last 6-12 months of the primary current account
- **Ownership proof** — Partnership deed, MOA/AOA, or proprietorship declaration

For self-employed professionals such as doctors and chartered accountants, a valid practice certificate and degree may substitute for some business documents.

## How Lenders Assess Your Repayment Capacity

Beyond ticking eligibility boxes, lenders run deeper checks to gauge whether you can comfortably repay.

**Banking behaviour.** They scan your bank statements for average balances, cheque bounces, and the ratio of credits to debits. Frequent returns of ECS or cheques are a red flag.

**FOIR / obligations.** Your existing EMIs and obligations are weighed against income. A high fixed-obligation-to-income ratio reduces the amount you can borrow. Before applying, use our [EMI Calculator](/calculators/emi-calculator) to model a comfortable EMI.

**GST turnover consistency.** Many lenders now pull GST returns directly. Wildly fluctuating or declining turnover invites scrutiny.

**Credit history.** A clean repayment record on past loans and credit cards is essential. Check yours for free before applying on any bureau's website.

## Secured vs Unsecured Business Loans

You broadly have two routes.

An **unsecured business loan** requires no collateral, has faster disbursal (sometimes within 48-72 hours), but carries higher interest and smaller ticket sizes. It suits working-capital needs and short-term gaps.

A **secured business loan** — such as a loan against property or machinery — offers larger amounts, longer tenures and lower rates, but demands collateral and a longer processing timeline. If you own property in Hyderabad or Bengaluru, a secured route can substantially cut your cost of borrowing. Learn more about a dedicated [Business Loan](/loans/business-loan) and its structures.

## Government Schemes Worth Knowing

Indian MSMEs have access to several credit schemes that improve eligibility:

- **CGTMSE** — Collateral-free credit up to ₹5 crore under a credit guarantee cover, useful if you lack security.
- **MUDRA loans** — Shishu, Kishore and Tarun categories fund micro units up to ₹10 lakh.
- **Stand-Up India** — Supports SC/ST and women entrepreneurs with loans from ₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore.
- **PMEGP** — Offers a margin-money subsidy for new manufacturing and service ventures.

These schemes are especially relevant for first-generation entrepreneurs and units in tier-2 towns across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana.

## Common Reasons for Rejection

Understanding why applications fail helps you avoid the same traps.

1. **Low or thin credit history** — no prior loans means lenders have little data to trust.
2. **Frequent cheque bounces** in bank statements.
3. **Mismatch between declared income and ITR / GST filings.**
4. **Too many recent loan enquiries**, signalling credit hunger.
5. **Negative business profile** — certain speculative or restricted sectors are avoided.

## Tips to Improve Your Eligibility

- Maintain a CIBIL score above 720 by paying every EMI and card bill on time.
- File ITR consistently and declare income that reflects your actual turnover.
- Keep your GST filings current and turnover trending upward.
- Reduce existing high-cost debt before applying, or consider a [balance transfer](/balance-transfer) to free up cash flow.
- Build a relationship with your bank through a well-run current account.

## Key Takeaways

- Business loan eligibility hinges on vintage, turnover, profitability and a CIBIL score of 700+.
- NBFCs and fintech lenders are more flexible than banks but price risk higher.
- Keep KYC, GST, Udyam, ITR and 6-12 months of bank statements ready before applying.
- Government schemes like CGTMSE and MUDRA help collateral-light and first-time borrowers.
- Clean banking behaviour and consistent GST/ITR filings are as important as the score itself.
- Check your credit report and model your EMI before you formally apply to avoid needless rejections.
