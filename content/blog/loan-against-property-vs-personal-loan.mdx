## Two Very Different Ways to Raise Funds

When you need a large sum, for business expansion, a medical emergency, a child's education abroad or debt consolidation, two options dominate: a loan against property (LAP) and a personal loan. One is secured against your real estate; the other is unsecured. The right choice depends on how much you need, how fast, and how much risk you can accept.

This guide compares them clearly so business owners and salaried borrowers across Hyderabad, AP, Telangana and beyond can decide.

## The Core Difference

A **loan against property** is a secured loan where you pledge a residential or commercial property you own. Because the lender holds collateral, rates are lower and loan amounts higher, but processing takes longer and your property is at risk.

A **personal loan** is unsecured. You get funds based on your income and credit profile alone, quickly and with no collateral, but at higher interest and smaller amounts.

## Side-by-Side Comparison

| Feature | Loan Against Property | Personal Loan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Security | Property pledged | None |
| Interest rate | Lower (indicative 9% to 12%) | Higher (11% to 24%) |
| Loan amount | Up to 60% to 70% of property value | Usually up to ₹40 lakh, income-linked |
| Tenure | Long, up to 15 years | Short, 1 to 5 years |
| Disbursal speed | Slower, days to weeks | Fast, sometimes same day |
| Risk on default | Loss of property | Credit damage, legal recovery |
| Processing | Property valuation, legal check | Minimal documentation |

## When Loan Against Property Wins

Choose LAP when:

- **You need a large amount**, say ₹20 lakh or more, that a personal loan cannot cover.
- **You want a low EMI** through a lower rate and longer tenure.
- **The purpose is long-term**, like business capital or funding a big project.
- **You own property** with clear, marketable title and are comfortable pledging it.

Because the rate is much lower and the tenure longer, monthly EMIs on LAP are far gentler on cash flow. Test the difference on our [EMI calculator](/calculators/emi-calculator).

## When a Personal Loan Wins

Choose a personal loan when:

- **You need money fast**, within a day or two.
- **The amount is moderate**, comfortably within your income-based eligibility.
- **You do not want to risk your property**, or you do not own one to pledge.
- **The need is short-term**, repaid within a few years.

## A Worked Comparison

Suppose Anil, who runs a business in Hyderabad, needs ₹25,00,000 for expansion.

| Option | Rate | Tenure | Approx. EMI | Total interest |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LAP | 10% | 10 years | ₹33,038 | ₹14.6 lakh |
| Personal loan | 16% | 5 years | ₹60,796 | ₹11.5 lakh |

The personal loan actually costs less total interest here because of the shorter tenure, but its EMI is nearly double, which may strain cash flow. LAP offers a much lower EMI and a bigger amount, at the cost of pledging property and paying more interest over a longer period. The right pick depends on whether Anil prioritises affordability per month or total cost, and his appetite for risk.

## Risk: The Deciding Factor

The single biggest difference is what happens if you cannot repay:

- **LAP default** can lead to the lender selling your property to recover dues, a serious consequence.
- **Personal loan default** damages your CIBIL score and invites legal recovery, but you do not lose an asset.

Never pledge property for a purpose you are unsure about. Keep a repayment cushion.

## Eligibility and Documentation

**Loan against property** needs:

- Property title deeds and chain documents
- Property valuation and legal verification
- Income proof (ITR, GST returns, bank statements)
- KYC

**Personal loan** needs:

- KYC and address proof
- Salary slips or ITR
- Bank statements

If you are a business owner weighing LAP, our checklist of [documents needed for a business loan](/loans/business-loan) covers most of what lenders ask for.

## The Role of Your Credit Score

Both products reward a strong CIBIL score with lower rates, but a personal loan depends on it far more heavily since there is no collateral. A score above 750 widens your options and cuts your rate. Check yours on any bureau's website before applying.

## A Simple Decision Framework

1. **Is the amount large and the need long-term?** Lean toward LAP.
2. **Do you need money within days and only a moderate sum?** A personal loan fits.
3. **Are you comfortable risking your property?** If not, avoid LAP regardless of the lower rate.
4. **Which matters more, a low monthly EMI or the lowest total interest?** LAP usually wins on EMI; a shorter personal loan can win on total cost.

## Key Takeaways

- Loan against property is secured, cheaper per month and allows larger amounts and longer tenures, but risks your property.
- Personal loans are unsecured, fast and collateral-free, but carry higher rates and smaller limits.
- Choose LAP for large, long-term needs and a low EMI; choose a personal loan for quick, moderate, short-term funding.
- The decisive factor is risk: LAP default can cost you your property.
- A strong CIBIL score lowers your rate on both, especially the unsecured personal loan.
